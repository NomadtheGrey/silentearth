# Silent Earth — Recipes for Craftables

Written 2026-10-25, at Adam's ask ("build out recipes for our craftables
now that we have harvestables").

STATUS: prototype recipes are KEPT verbatim. Every new recipe is DRAFT for
Adam's pin pass — numbers stay draft until he pins them, per standing rule.
Tool upgrades are priced as UPGRADE COSTS (what it takes to evolve the tool
you already have), not full rebuilds. Station tags follow the
workstation/fire split: workstation = no-fire work, fire = fire-required
work, hand = made anywhere.

## Kept prototype recipes (verbatim from world_data.json)

- **Garden Trowel** — Snap-Wood 4 + Twined Cordage 2 + River Stone 2.
  At a workstation. (She creates it; it upgrades as she goes.)
- **Primitive Lean-to** — Snap-Wood 6 + Tender-Vines 3 + Peat Moss 2.
- **Wattle-and-Daub Hut** — Downed Oak 4 + River Silt 12 + Tender-Vines 8.
  (Ancestor of the merged Woven Cottage below.)
- **Woven Cottage (prototype)** — Downed Oak 10 + Twined Cordage 6 +
  Peat Moss 15 + River Stone 4, was workstation-gated. (Superseded by the
  merged recipe below; workstation requirement removed.)
- **Moss Cradle** — Luminous Spores 3 + River Silt 4 + Peat Moss 6.
  Garden plot, parked until crops exist.

## Tool ladder — upgrade costs (DRAFT)

- **Sharp Trowel** (sharpen the garden trowel) — River Stone as the
  whetstone, at a workstation. RESOLVED (Adam, 2026-10-25): no conversion,
  no loss — the stone is kept, sharpening costs nothing consumable.
- **Spade** (route A middle, from sharp trowel) — Snap-Wood 4 +
  River Stone 2 + Twined Cordage 2. At a workstation.
- **Axe-Trowel** (route B middle, from sharp trowel) — Snap-Wood 4 +
  River Stone 4 + Twined Cordage 2. At a workstation. (The axe edge is
  stone — bog iron comes later, post-axe.)
- **Spade-Axe** (convergence, from EITHER middle) — Snap-Wood 4 +
  River Stone 2 + Twined Cordage 2. At a workstation. (Same delta either
  road; the two routes cost the same to converge.)
- **Trundle-Tool** (wheeled form, from Spade-Axe) — Snap-Wood 6 +
  Twined Cordage 4 + River Stone 2. At a workstation. (Wood wheels and
  frame, cordage lashing, stone axle/pivot.) Moves stations.

## Fire ladder (DRAFT)

- **Campfire** — Snap-Wood 3 + Dry Kindling 2. Flint optional: with flint,
