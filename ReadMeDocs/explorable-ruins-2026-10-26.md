# Silent Earth — Explorable Ruins (picked, 2026-10-26)

Status: PICKED by Adam 2026-10-26 ("I like all those ideas, let's have those be 'explorable ruins' in other places"). Future content — NOT MVP. Ruins are explorable (she can enter them and pick up old things) and placed in other places across the world (exact spots TBD, not at spawn). REGION PINNED 2026-10-26: Great Lakes / Upper Midwest (Michigan, Wisconsin, northern Indiana/Ohio). Enterable detail (Adam 2026-10-26, clarified "rubble-fied"): later, ruins will be enterable — a ruin is enterable if it is NOT completely rubble-fied (fully collapsed = rubble only, not enterable; partially intact = enterable). Entering an enterable place: she "appears" in what amounts to a new zone for exploration (transition appearance, not continuous walk-through; implementation TBD).

Loot shape (from the brainstorm): old things of varying usefulness — she picks things up and figures out what's treasure and what's just heavy. Sorting useful from junk is part of the fun.

## The seven

- **Drowned mill on the creek** — millstone (deadfall counterweight, anyone?), rusted gears, waterlogged ledgers with one page somehow still readable.
- **Farmhouse on the oldfield rise** — canned goods (some fine, some bulging — varying usefulness, literally), quilts, hand tools.
- **Sunken road with car husks** — wire, mirrors, glass, license plates. Half treasure, half junk.
- **One-room schoolhouse** — books (some ruined, one herbal that's actually useful), chalk, a globe, a bell that is far too heavy and therefore funny.
- **Chapel on the meadow** — candles already there, hymnals, a bell. Folk horror with a wink.
- **General store** — jars, tins, one still-good wrench, and a whole lot of rust.
- **Rail siding boxcar** — crates, rope, and a lantern. Light finds light.
