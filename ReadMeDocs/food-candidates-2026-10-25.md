# Silent Earth — Food Candidates (brainstorm, 2026-10-25)

NOT pins — Adam's picks still to come. Candidates for the food need, which the harvestables check found entirely missing. Shaped by two rulings: survival first with magic strongly hinted (2026-10-25), and some foods doubling as reagents when the magic layer opens (Adam, 2026-10-25).

## Tuning shape (proposed, not pinned)

- **Many kinds, small amounts.** A swamp is generous in variety, poor in bulk: a frog's legs, a handful of berries, one crayfish. She forages often, never feasts.
- **Preparation-gated.** Toads and newts are toxic raw; snails need purging and cooking; mushrooms must be identified. Knowledge is the survival skill — very witch.
- **Weather-gated and seasonal.** Cold rain days cut foraging; October abundance fades toward winter. Natural difficulty ramp, no new system needed.

## Candidates by where

**The wet half (creek, hollow, mud):**
- Crayfish / crawdads — creek, cooked. Safe, small, plentiful.
- Frogs — creek edges, cooked. The plain staple.
- Toads — mud. Toxic raw, must be prepared. **Dual-purpose: folk-magic reagent (toad toxin/bone).**
- Newts — shallow water/mud. Toxic raw, must be prepared. **Dual-purpose: the iconic reagent — "eye of newt."**
- Snails — damp hollow. Purged + cooked. Slow food, thematically perfect.
- Eels — creek. Rich food, harder to catch. Optional.

**The wooded half (thicket, deepwood):**
- Bog berries — cranberry-type, thicket edges. Safe staple, autumn-limited (fades into winter — the seasonal ramp).
- Elderberries — thicket. Must be cooked (raw berries sicken) — preparation-gated.
- Acorns — deepwood. Must be leached/ground — labor-intensive staple.
- Mushrooms — deepwood/hollow. **Split: safe edibles vs toxic lookalikes (identification risk — eating wrong = sickness, which is also the medicine need's origin story). Fly agaric: toxic AND the classic witch reagent — dual-purpose.**
- Nightshade berries — deepwood, rare. Poisonous as food, potent as reagent. **Dual-purpose, high risk/high hint.**
- Bird eggs — thicket, seasonal. Small, spring/summer only — mostly gone by October; scarcity storytelling.

## The bridge (Adam's point, 2026-10-25)

The dual-purpose foods are how the magic layer arrives honestly: she already knows these things as food (or as things to avoid) before she learns them as reagents. The Primal Bone description hints; the newt, the toad, the fly agaric pay the hint off. Today's pantry is tomorrow's ingredient shelf — no new system, just a second use discovered later.
