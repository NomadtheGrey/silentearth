# Silent Earth — Carrying Capacity

Written 2026-10-25. Personal carrying (on her person) is a different system from
home storage (homes hold supplies safe from the elements) and from station
hauling (the Trundle-Tool moves stations). Keep the three distinct.

## The ladder (Adam, 2026-10-25)

A ladder of increasing personal pocket as she upgrades.

- **Rung 1: POCKETS (starting).** What she carries on her person at game start.
  BASE CAPACITY (Adam, 2026-10-25): 10.
- **Early rung: HAND BASKET (Adam, 2026-10-25 — "in there someplace early
  on").** A hand basket joins the ladder early. BONUS (Adam, 2026-10-25):
  +25 as the SMALL BASKET (recorded as the same item unless Adam says
  otherwise; "small" leaves room for a bigger basket later).
- **APRON (Adam, 2026-10-25): +10.** A carrying container; adds 10 to
  capacity under the additive rule.
- **LARGE BASKET, upgrade (Adam, 2026-10-25): 50.** A bigger basket,
  marked as an upgrade of the small basket. OPEN: does it REPLACE the
  small basket's +25 (total 70) or STACK on top of it (total 95)?

## Rulings (Adam, 2026-10-25)

- **Trundle-Tool: technically separate from inventory, but a hefty bonus —
  PINNED +50 (Adam, 2026-10-26).**
  The Trundle-Tool is not part of the personal inventory system — it is its
  own thing (the station hauler) — but it provides a +50 bonus to carrying
  capacity.
- **Additive rule.** When a new carrying thing is created (apron, basket,
  etc.), it simply ADDS to carrying capacity. Stacking, not replacing — no
  swapping one container for a better one, no complex slot system. Capacity
  = pockets base (10) + apron (+10) + small basket (+25) + large basket
  (50; replace-vs-stack open) + ... (+ Trundle-Tool's +50 bonus, pinned
  2026-10-26).
- **Brainstorm liked.** Adam liked Marlowe's carrying brainstorm in general
  (2026-10-25). General approval, not individual pins — each mechanic
  (size-based capacity, overfill-but-slow, arm-carry, floating basket) is
  still to be picked on its own.
- **Arm-carry until the Trundle-Tool — PARKED for now (Adam, 2026-10-25).** She can
  arm-carry (one bulky thing, in her arms, slow) UNTIL she gets the
  Trundle-Tool; then the tool takes over and arm-carrying ends. (This
  picks the arm-carry brainstorm idea with that condition.)
  REFINED (Adam, 2026-10-25): arm-carry covers only a FEW things —
  logs (named) + a couple other things (TBD), not anything bulky. PARKED for now (not cut; revisit later).

## Brainstorm (Marlowe, 2026-10-25 — none picked)

- **Size-based capacity, not just count.** Pockets hold small things only
  (flint, spores, berries); the basket holds bulky things (wood, moss). The
  ladder is about WHAT she can carry, not just how much.
- **Overfill-but-slow (soft cap).** She CAN cram over capacity, but moves
  slow — same slow language as the station-carrying rule, friendlier than a
  hard "you can't pick that up."
- **More rungs:** apron (with big pockets), waist pouch, satchel, gathering
  apron (real foraging gear — a big front pocket for harvest, very witch),
  burlap sack, shoulder bundle/sling.
- **Arm-carry for one bulky thing.** One oak log, one big item, carried in
  her arms (slow) — separate from pocket/basket, parallel to the station
  rule.
- **Floating basket (absurdity).** Drop a basket in the creek and it floats —
  a touch of absurdity, and maybe a real mechanic (basket as a little boat
  for goods?).
- **Trundle-Tool as the top rung.** The personal ladder tops out; beyond it,
  the wheeled tool hauls the rest. Personal → tool → home is the full
  carrying arc.
