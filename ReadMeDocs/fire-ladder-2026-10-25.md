# Silent Earth — Fire Ladder (Adam, 2026-10-25)

Fire is an upgrade path, parallel to the tool ladder and the housing ladder.

## The ladder

1. **Campfire** — the start. Open-fire cooking (stick, or chucked in the fire and dug out), warmth, light.
- **FIRE-STARTING (Adam, 2026-10-25):** FLINT is a new findable item — NOT required for the first campfire, but easier. With flint: click...click...click...WOOF! (fast, a few interactions). Without flint (sticks/friction): rub...rub...rub... (wait 20–30 seconds)...rub... (slow, tedious). Principle: convenience, not gate — she can always make fire; flint just makes it fast. SOURCE (Adam, 2026-10-25): flint lies around WHEREVER (ubiquitous surface find) — cities were post-flint-dependent (nobody scrounging even before the fall) + ~60 years post-apocalypse (nobody digging for it lately).
2. **Hearth** — better/more food from cooking. Cooking improves at this stage (more food, better food — exact numbers TBD, tuning later).
3. **Kiln** — specifically for firing clay. This is where pottery (receptacles) and bricks get fired. Fire-required work, per the workstation/fire split (workstation = no-fire raw ingredients; fire = fire-required work).
4. **Bloomery** — the smelting stage. WORD SUPPLIED (Marlowe, 2026-10-25; Adam to veto): "bloomery" is the historical term for the small furnace that smelts bog iron into a bloom of wrought iron; "forge" is the common-word alternative. For smelting BOG IRON, for the NEXT tool upgrade (post-axe-evolution).

## New resource: bog iron

- **Bog iron** — iron ore that forms in swampy ground. A natural swamp resource, and the material for the next tool upgrade after the axe evolution. Exact source mechanic TBD (dug? harvested? where in the bog?).
- **Chain:** find bog iron → smelt it in the bloomery → next tool upgrade. The fire ladder gates the tool ladder at this stage, just as the kiln gates pottery and bricks.

## How the ladders interlock

- **Tool ladder:** garden trowel → sharp trowel (whetstone) → axe evolution → iron tool (bog iron, name TBD).
- **Housing ladder:** lean-to → thatch hut → woven cottage (wattle-and-daub) → small wooden hut.
- **Fire ladder:** campfire → hearth → kiln → bloomery.
- Each ladder's stage gates the next thing she can make: kiln gates pottery/bricks, bloomery gates iron tools, housing gates storage capacity and elemental protection.
